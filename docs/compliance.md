# Compliance rules this system checks

Checked 11 October 2026. `/compliance` runs every rule below against the records and reports breaches first. The rules are what the law says about commission once it is paid; this system never pays anyone, never files with the ATO or IRD and never replaces payroll. Nothing here is legal or tax advice: confirm each rule with your adviser before you rely on it, and update this file and the check together when a rule changes.

| Rule | What the check looks for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **AU: commission is ordinary time earnings, so super is due on it.** Section 6(1) of the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 includes commissions in ordinary time earnings; SGR 2009/2 explains how. | A paid commission to an Australian employee with no super payment recorded. | [ATO: SGR 2009/2](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=SGR/SGR20092/NAT/ATO/00001), [ATO: calculating super guarantee](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/super-guarantee-employer-obligations-online-course/module-4-calculating-super-guarantee) |
| **AU: the super guarantee rate is 12%.** In force since 1 July 2025. | An Australian employee whose super rate on commission is below 12%. | [ATO: calculating super guarantee](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/super-guarantee-employer-obligations-online-course/module-4-calculating-super-guarantee) |
| **AU: Payday Super.** From 1 July 2026 the fund must receive the super within 7 business days of each payday (Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025). Some cases get 20 business days (new employees, out-of-cycle payments). | Super recorded as reaching the fund more than 7 business days after the period's payday, or not recorded at all once 7 business days have passed. Public holidays are not counted out; treat a result of 8 as "check the calendar". | [ATO: Payday Super compliance approach](https://www.ato.gov.au/pdscompliance), [Hall & Wilcox summary](https://hallandwilcox.com.au/news/payday-super-starts-1-july-2026-what-employers-need-to-do-now/) |
| **AU: pay records show how commission was worked out.** Fair Work Regulations 2009 reg 3.33(3) requires details of incentive-based payments; records are kept for 7 years (Fair Work Act 2009 s535). | A payout to an Australian payee with no statement lines. Payouts and their lines cannot be edited or deleted once a period locks (enforced in the database). | [Fair Work Ombudsman: record-keeping](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/rights-and-obligations/record-keeping), [HopgoodGanim on reg 3.33](https://www.hopgoodganim.com.au/news-insights/fair-work-regulations-record-keeping-and-pay-slips/) |
| **NZ: commission is gross earnings for holiday pay.** Under the Holidays Act 2003, most commissions count in gross earnings and so in average weekly earnings for annual holiday pay. | A paid commission to a New Zealand employee where the period has no payroll reference, meaning it may have been paid outside payroll and missed the holiday pay calculation. | [Employment New Zealand: gross earnings](https://employment.govt.nz/leave-and-holidays/calculating-payments-for-leave-and-holidays/gross-earnings/), [Employment New Zealand: calculating holiday pay](https://www.employment.govt.nz/pay-and-hours/pay-and-wages/leave-and-holiday-pay/calculating-holiday-and-leave-pay) |
| **Local rule: a written plan, accepted before pay.** Not a statute. Most commission disputes start with a plan nobody signed. | A payee being paid in an open period against a plan they have not accepted. | Your own policy. Change or remove it with `/customise`. |

## Reported, not checked

- **AASB 15 and NZ IFRS 15, paragraphs 91 to 94.** Commission is an incremental cost of obtaining a contract. Where the contract runs longer than a year it is usually capitalised and expensed over the contract; a year or less may be expensed straight away (the practical expedient in paragraph 94). `/contract-costs` lists the capitalised amount and monthly expense for every paid deal over 12 months. It is a schedule for your accountant, not a journal. Source: [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/).
- **KiwiSaver.** Commission is part of the gross pay KiwiSaver contributions are worked out on. The payee's rate is stored and the contribution is shown on each statement; payroll makes and files the contribution.
- **State payroll tax (AU).** Commission paid to employees counts as wages for payroll tax, and some commission contractors are caught by the contractor provisions. Payroll tax is filed from payroll; ask your adviser about any contractor on commission.

## What this system does not do

It does not calculate PAYE or PAYG withholding, does not make super or KiwiSaver payments, does not lodge Single Touch Payroll or payday filing, and does not hold money. Those stay in payroll.
